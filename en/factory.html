Inside Our Factory

Haogeng is the export arm of Guigeng Agricultural Machinery, and every machine on this site is built in our own plant. Below is what the workshop looks like — the equipment, the assembly work, and the packing we do before a machine leaves.

Get a Quote

Our own manufacturing plant

All of our machines are built at the Guangxi Lichuang plant of the same group. We do not buy machines from other factories and re-label them, and there is no trading company between the plant and you.

Metal fabrication and welding

CNC laser cutting, CNC bending, laser pipe cutting and laser welding produce the frames, handles and gearbox housings. Working the metal inside the plant is what keeps the fit between parts consistent from one batch to the next.

Assembly

Frame, engine, gearbox and handle are fitted and checked one machine at a time. The gearbox is the part that carries the daily work, so it is the part we look at most closely.

Packing and loading

Each machine is packed with the attachments it is sold with, and the packed machine is protected for container transport, so it arrives without handling damage.

Patents and promoted-product status

Our R&D holds national utility-model patents, and our flagship 4WD micro tiller is listed by China's agricultural machinery authority as a National Agricultural Machinery Promoted Product. Certificate details are available on request.
