186 Diesel Tiller (Beast)

Model: 1WG-6.3B
📋 Specifications

Overview

Beast 186cc diesel micro tiller (1WG-6-3B) delivers 6.3 kW / 5-15 HP @ 3600 rpm with 900-1200 mm adjustable tilling width at 180-350 mm depth. Reinforced ductile-iron gearbox and diesel engine optimized for heavy-duty commercial farms. Recoil start, adjustable handlebar, full accessory compatibility.

    Features

      What's in the Box

      All accessories shown below are included FREE when you purchase this machine — ready to use straight out of the crate.

      1. 1
        Paddy Field Wheel Set×1 set
        Wide paddle wheels designed for soft, waterlogged paddy fields. Slip directly onto the rotor shaft in place of the dry-land blades when working in wet conditions.
      2. 2
        Anti-Wrap Blade Set×1 set
        Curved blades engineered to resist weed and vine wrap. The swept geometry keeps tall grass and crop residue from tangling around the rotor during heavy tillage.
      3. 3
        Rubber Tire Set×1 set
        Treaded rubber transport wheels with metal hubs and a square axle bore. Swap on when you need to roll the machine between plots without scarring the ground.
      4. 4
        Dry-Land Blade Set×1 set
        Hardened H-pattern rotary blades for breaking firm, dry soil. Pre-assembled on the cage frame, ready to mount on the rotor shaft straight out of the crate.
