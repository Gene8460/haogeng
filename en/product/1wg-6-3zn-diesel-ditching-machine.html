186 Ditching Machine (Bull Pro)

Model: 1WG-6.3ZN
📋 Specifications

Overview

186Cc Diesel ditching-ridger attachment (1WG-6-3ZN) delivers 6.3 kW / 8.4 HP for digging 350-600 mm furrows in one pass. Converts any compatible micro tiller into a dedicated ridger. Reinforced ductile-iron gearbox handles heavy clay and rocky soil. Same recoil start, adjustable handlebar, full compatibility with the 1WG.6.3ZN tiller base.

Best For

  • Heavy clay or rocky soil farms
  • Potato, tomato, pepper row cultivation
  • Orchard drainage and irrigation channels
  • Commercial farms 2+ ha
  • Raised-bed greenhouse preparation

Features

  • 186Cc Diesel 4-stroke air-cooled — 6.3 kW / 8.4 HP
  • Ditching depth: 350-600 mm
  • Adjustable ditching width 200-400 mm
  • Direct-drive rotary tiller + ridger combo
  • Ductile-iron gearbox, 2 forward + 1 reverse
  • Recoil start — no battery
  • Net weight: 140 kg
  • Tool-free attachment swap
  • Adjustable handlebar (height + horizontal)
  • Compatible with all 1WG-6 series tiller bases
